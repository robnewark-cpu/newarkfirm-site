# Video Scripts — ready to record

The site now has **video slots + `VideoObject` schema** built into every
practice page (inert until a real video URL is added). These are short scripts
you (or Rob) can shoot on a phone — no studio needed. A talking-head attorney
video builds trust and is eligible for video rich results + AI citation.

## How to publish a recorded video
1. Record the video (phone is fine; good light, quiet room, landscape 16:9).
2. Upload to YouTube (unlisted or public) or your host; copy the **embed URL**.
3. In the relevant page's content spec, set `video={...}` with `title`, `url`
   (embed URL), `description`, `thumbnail`, `upload_date`, then rebuild.
4. The page renders the embed + `VideoObject` schema automatically.

## Compliance
- Keep it informational; no outcome promises, no "I will win your case."
- End with the standard disclaimer line (below). These are attorney advertising.
- Standard close: *"This is general information, not legal advice, and does not
  create an attorney-client relationship. Every case is different."*

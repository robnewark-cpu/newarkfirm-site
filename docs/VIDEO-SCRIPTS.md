# Video Scripts — tool-made explainers (no attorney on camera)

All six videos below are designed to be produced with a video tool (Canva,
Descript, Pictory, or similar) as **motion-graphic + voiceover informational
explainers**. No real or simulated attorney appears; no specific person is
implied. This keeps them appropriate for a law firm and compliant with Texas
and Oklahoma attorney-advertising rules.

## Compliance rules (MANDATORY — build these into every video)
- **No fabricated content.** No statistics, success rates, settlement amounts,
  outcomes, client stories, reviews, awards, or rankings. Only general legal
  information (how a process works).
- **No simulated attorney / deepfake.** Do not use an AI avatar that looks like
  a specific lawyer, and do not have a synthetic "attorney" give advice. Use
  neutral motion graphics, text, and b-roll (courthouse, documents, city) with
  a neutral voiceover.
- **No promises.** Never "we will win," "guaranteed," "you will get X."
- **On-screen "Attorney Advertising" label** for at least the first few seconds.
- **Closing disclaimer on screen AND in voiceover** (see below).
- **Firm voice, not first person.** "At Newark Law Offices…" / "Here's how it
  works" — never "Hi, I'm [name]."
- Applicable rules: Texas Disciplinary Rules of Professional Conduct 7.01–7.06;
  Oklahoma Rules of Professional Conduct 7.1–7.5 (no false or misleading
  communications; advertising must be identifiable as such).

**Standard closing card (all videos), on screen + voiceover:**
> "Newark Law Offices — Texas & Oklahoma. Free consultation: 866-230-7236.
> Attorney advertising. This is general information, not legal advice, and does
> not create an attorney-client relationship. Prior results do not guarantee a
> similar outcome."

## Production notes
- Format: 16:9, 30–60 sec, captions burned in (most viewers watch muted).
- Neutral stock/b-roll only (no footage implying a specific client or result).
- Upload to YouTube → copy embed URL → set `video={...}` on the page spec → rebuild.

## After production
Add each finished video to its page by setting the `video` field in the page's
content spec (title, embed url, description, thumbnail, upload_date) and running
`python3 build/build.py`. The page renders the embed plus `VideoObject` schema
automatically. Nothing appears on the site until a real video URL is added.

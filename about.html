About Newark Law Offices

A boutique Texas and Oklahoma firm built around a focused case model: fewer matters, more direct attorney attention, and a strategic approach to litigation, corporate advocacy, and transactional work.

Our Approach

We built Newark Law Offices around the idea that clients — whether corporate, financial, or individual — are best served by a smaller, more accountable team rather than being one file among hundreds. Every matter is directed by Robert C. Newark, III, with support from a focused team across our Dallas and Edmond offices.

Executive-level attention

Direct access to the attorney handling your matter, not a rotating cast of associates.

Cross-border capability

Attorneys licensed in Texas, Oklahoma, Alaska, Wisconsin, and South Dakota as listed on Our People, plus named federal courts. Local counsel is case-by-case. Alaska, Wisconsin, and South Dakota originating matters are referral-basis.

Business-minded counsel

Litigation and transactional strategy shaped around the client’s commercial goals, not just the docket.

Office hours & fees

Business hours

Both the Dallas and Edmond offices are open Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central Time. Phone calls and online case reviews are handled during business hours. In-office meetings are by appointment only.

Fee structure

Initial case review is free and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Personal injury and wind/hail claims may use a contingent fee in appropriate cases. Litigation, corporate, bankruptcy, and local-counsel work is typically hourly, flat, or retainer-based. All terms are set out in a written agreement.

Common questions

Does submitting a form or using the chat create an attorney-client relationship?
No. Intake, chat, and this website are for general information and case-review routing only until the firm is formally engaged.
Where should I start if I have a deadline?
Call 866-230-7236. You can also check case eligibility online. Do not wait on a form if a sale, answer date, or limitation period is imminent.
Where can I read about how these matters usually work?
See Insights for practice notes and court briefings. Practice notes explain how a process usually runs. Briefings go up when a court or statute actually moves a file. That material is general information, not legal advice.
Do you practice only in Texas and Oklahoma?
Offices and primary dockets are Dallas and Edmond. Robert C. Newark, III is licensed in Texas and Oklahoma, with federal admissions in Texas, Oklahoma, Colorado, New Mexico, and Arkansas. Tyler Wilson is licensed in Oklahoma, Alaska, Wisconsin, and South Dakota. Alaska, Wisconsin, and South Dakota originating matters are referral-basis. We also act as local counsel case-by-case in licensed courts.
What are your business hours?
Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central Time at both offices. Phone and online case review during business hours. In-office visits by appointment. See the Contact Hub for details.
How much does a consultation or attorney cost?
Initial case review is free and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Personal injury and wind/hail claims may use a contingent fee in appropriate cases. Litigation, corporate, bankruptcy, and local-counsel work is typically hourly, flat, or retainer-based. Fee terms are confirmed in a written agreement. See fees & initial review.

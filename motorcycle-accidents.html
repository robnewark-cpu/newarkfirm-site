Fighting the bias

Are motorcycle accident claims harder because of rider bias?

Insurers sometimes assume a motorcyclist was at fault or riding recklessly. A claim is decided on evidence — scene photos, witness accounts, the other driver's actions, and physical evidence — not on stereotypes. Motorcycle injuries tend to be severe, so valuing future medical care and lost earning capacity accurately is essential before any settlement.

What we document

  • The other driver's conduct (left turns, lane changes, failure to yield)
  • Scene and vehicle evidence, and any available video
  • The full medical picture, including future care
  • Every applicable policy, including UM/UIM

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay a fee if there is no recovery?
In appropriate cases these matters are handled on a contingent fee: the attorney fee is a percentage of any recovery, and no attorney fee is charged if there is no recovery. Case expenses are separate and are addressed in a written fee agreement.
Does not wearing a helmet hurt my claim?
It can become an argument, but it does not automatically bar recovery. Liability still turns on who caused the crash. Bring the facts to a review.

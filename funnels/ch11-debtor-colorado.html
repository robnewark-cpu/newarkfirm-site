Exhibit B — The Process

What filing actually looks like

  • 01

    Free case review

    We look at your debt structure, assets, and pressure points to determine whether Chapter 11, Subchapter V, or another path fits.

  • 02

    Pre-filing strategy

    We identify priority creditors, prepare required schedules, and time the filing to maximize the benefit of the automatic stay.

  • 03

    Petition & automatic stay

    Filing immediately halts most collection actions, lawsuits, and repossessions against you or your business.

  • 04

    Plan of reorganization

    We negotiate with creditors and draft a plan to restructure debt on terms your business can actually meet.

  • 05

    Confirmation & emergence

    Once the court confirms the plan, you operate under its terms and exit bankruptcy with a sustainable balance sheet.

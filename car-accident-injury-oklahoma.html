Exhibit A — The Situation

Signs your claim needs an attorney, not just an adjuster

If any of this describes your accident in Oklahoma:

  • 01

    The insurer's offer doesn't cover your medical bills

    Initial offers are often calculated to close the file, not to cover your actual treatment and recovery.

  • 02

    The other driver's insurer is disputing fault

    Comparative fault disputes can cut your recovery significantly if you don't push back with evidence.

  • 03

    You're still being treated and don't know your total damages

    Settling before treatment is complete usually means settling for less than the claim is worth.

  • 04

    You've lost income or suffered a lasting injury

    Lost wages, future medical care, and pain and suffering all belong in a properly valued claim — insurers won't calculate these for you.

Exhibit B — The Process

How we build your claim

  • 01

    Free case review

    We review the accident, your injuries, and the insurance coverage involved to tell you plainly what your claim is likely worth.

  • 02

    Investigation

    Police reports, medical records, witness statements, and (where needed) accident reconstruction build the record for your claim.

  • 03

    Demand & negotiation

    We send a demand backed by evidence and negotiate directly with the insurer — not the other way around.

  • 04

    Litigation if needed

    Where the insurer won't offer a fair settlement, we file suit and take the case to court.

  • 05

    Resolution

    Settlement or verdict, the goal is full and fair value for your injury, not just a quick check.

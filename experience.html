Experience

A broad litigation, corporate, and transactional practice built to handle the matters Texas and Oklahoma businesses and individuals face most often.

Corporate & Commercial Matters

Our core practice centers on representing companies in corporate litigation defense, advising banks and lenders through our financial institution advisory work, and guiding businesses through complex civil litigation and dispute resolution. For companies operating on both sides of the Red River, we also handle Texas–Oklahoma corporate transactions from diligence through closing.

Bankruptcy & Creditor-Debtor Matters

We represent both sides of Chapter 11 proceedings — creditors seeking to protect a claim through Chapter 11 creditor representation in Texas, and businesses pursuing reorganization through debtor reorganization in Texas or in Oklahoma. We also assist homeowners facing foreclosure in Texas or in Oklahoma.

Personal Injury & Property Claims

On the individual side, we review car-accident injury claims from the personal injury hub — including Texas and Oklahoma intakes — along with wind and hail insurance disputes in Texas and in Oklahoma, for clients facing underpaid or denied claims.
